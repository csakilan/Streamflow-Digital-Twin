What changed, and what remains
  • Replaced the default blocky flood-fill depiction with a mapped reference surface.
  • Added mapped/original/imagery controls and optional real bank outlines.
  • Separated date-bound observations and discrete model forecasts from the live beacon and animation interpolation.
  • Marked rating-derived gauge heights as estimates; added separately sourced measured sample summaries.
  • Incomplete terrain or unavailable reference files hide mapped water and show its status.
  • Precise absolute water levels and moving flood boundaries still require datum reconciliation, channel/riverbed data and validation.
